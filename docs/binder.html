Live data from your backend API. A slot shows greyed-out art either because you don't own a copy of that card yet or because a copy exists but isn't filed into a collection. A greyed slot's own + button fills it from your collection once a matching copy is available — it's disabled until then. 3×3 binders fit raw/sleeved/toploader copies; 4×3 (penny sleeve style) fits raw/sleeved only — slabs and toploaders in a thin-page binder don't physically fit, so those copies can't be assigned to one. Use the 📘 button on Browse, Wishlist, or Collection to send a card here — even one you don't own yet. Click a filled slot to pick it up (it outlines in red), then click any other slot to move it there — swapping places if that slot was already filled. Click the 🔍 icon on a card to view it full-size.
